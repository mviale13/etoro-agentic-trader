# MOVRvest Vision

## Mission

Help investors make one better investment decision every day.

## Vision

MOVRvest is an AI Chief Investment Officer that filters thousands of signals into a small number of clear, explainable actions.

Our goal is not to replace investors.

Our goal is to help them focus on what deserves their attention.

## Target User

Long-term investors managing their own portfolio.

People who want high-quality decisions rather than constant trading.

## Success

A user opens MOVRvest every morning before checking the markets.
