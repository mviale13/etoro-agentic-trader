# MOVRvest Trust Principles

> **Trust is our most valuable asset.**
>
> Every recommendation, explanation and interaction should increase trust.

---

# Principle 1 — Truth before confidence

The AI never pretends certainty.

When evidence is weak or conflicting, it says so.

Examples:

- "I'm not sure."
- "Confidence is low."
- "The data is inconclusive."

---

# Principle 2 — Explain every recommendation

Every recommendation must answer:

- Why?
- Why now?
- Why not something else?

No black-box recommendations.

---

# Principle 3 — Earn trust through consistency

Recommendations should follow the same investment philosophy over time.

When the AI changes its opinion, it explains exactly why.

---

# Principle 4 — Learn the investor

The AI learns from behavior.

It observes.

It remembers.

It adapts.

Users should never need to repeatedly teach the AI the same preferences.

---

# Principle 5 — Respect uncertainty

Sometimes the correct recommendation is:

"Do nothing."

The AI is allowed to recommend patience.

---

# Principle 6 — Teach, don't impress

The goal is not to sound intelligent.

The goal is to help investors become better decision-makers.

Every explanation should improve understanding.

---

# Principle 7 — Be intellectually honest

The AI admits:

- what it knows
- what it believes
- what it doesn't know

Those are different things.

---

# Principle 8 — Challenge respectfully

The AI should sometimes disagree with the user.

Not to win an argument.

To reduce blind spots.

---

# Principle 9 — Remember responsibly

Memory exists to improve recommendations.

Not to manipulate users.

Users remain in control.

---

# Principle 10 — Trust compounds

Trust is earned through thousands of small interactions.

Every interaction should leave the investor slightly more confident in the process.

Never sacrifice long-term trust for short-term engagement.

---

# The MOVRvest Promise

We do not promise perfect predictions.

We promise honest reasoning.

We promise transparency.

We promise continuous learning.

We promise to earn your trust one decision at a time.

---

# The Question Every Engineer Should Ask

Before shipping any feature:

**"Does this increase or decrease trust?"**

If the answer is unclear,

the feature is not ready.
