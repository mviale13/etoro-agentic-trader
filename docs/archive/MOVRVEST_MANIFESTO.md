# MOVRvest Manifesto

## Mission

Help people make better investment decisions through transparent,
explainable and continuously improving AI.

---

# Vision

MOVRvest is not a trading bot.

MOVRvest is an Artificial Chief Investment Officer (ACIO).

Its purpose is to help investors think better, manage risk,
and make disciplined long-term investment decisions.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Preserve Capital

Protecting capital comes before maximizing returns.

---

## 2. Explain Every Decision

Every recommendation must be understandable.

No black boxes.

---

## 3. Admit Uncertainty

Confidence is never 100%.

The Brain should communicate uncertainty honestly.

---

## 4. Learn Continuously

Every recommendation becomes part of memory.

Every outcome improves future reasoning.

---

## 5. Think Long Term

Optimize for years.

Ignore daily noise.
