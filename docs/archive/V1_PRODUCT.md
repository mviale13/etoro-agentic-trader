# MOVRvest v1

## Mission

Help investors make one better investment decision every day.

---

# Morning Brief

The first screen every user sees.

The goal is to answer one question:

"What deserves my attention today?"

The Morning Brief should take less than 60 seconds to read.

---

## 1. Attention Score

Low / Moderate / High

Example:

🟡 Moderate

Three investment decisions require your attention today.

---

## 2. Top 3 Decisions

Instead of three BUY recommendations, the AI surfaces the three most important actions.

Example

🏆 Buy Microsoft

⚠ Review PayPal

💎 Discover Constellation Software

---

## 3. Why?

Every recommendation must be explainable.

No black-box AI.

---

## 4. Portfolio Health

Current score

Expected score after recommendations

---

## 5. Market Context

Only the events that matter today.

Example

Two earnings reports.

Fed speech.

Nothing else.
